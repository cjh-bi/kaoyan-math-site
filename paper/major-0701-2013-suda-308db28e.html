(4) $a_{n}^{*}$ 是单调递减的数列, 且 $a_{0}^{*}=\max \limits_{n \in N} a_{n}$;
(5) $\forall x \in(0,+\infty), \sharp\{n \in N: a_{n}>x\}=\sharp\left\{m \in N: a_{m}^{*}>x\right\}$.
