(4) 设 $g(x)$, $f(x, y)$ 均二阶可微, $u(x, y) = y g(\cos x) + f(e^x, xy)$, 求 $\dfrac{\partial u}{\partial x}$, $\dfrac{\partial^2 u}{\partial x \partial y}$.

$\oint_C \frac{(x + 4y)\mathrm{d}y + (x - y)\mathrm{d}x}{x^2 + 4y^2}.$

$\sum_{n=1}^{\infty} n! \left(\frac{-e}{n}\right)^n.$

(8) 设 $a_n > 0$, $\lim\limits_{n \to \infty} a_n = a > 0$, 讨论级数
$\sum_{n=1}^{\infty} \left(\frac{a}{a_n}\right)^n$
的敛散性.
