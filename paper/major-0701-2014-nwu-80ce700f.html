二、解答题.5 |5 个小问

(1) $\mathscr{A}^2 = \mathscr{A}$;
(2) $\mathscr{A}V = (\mathscr{E} - \mathscr{A})^{-1}(0)$, 即 $\mathscr{A}$ 的值域等于 $\mathscr{E} - \mathscr{A}$ 的核;
(3) $V = \mathscr{A}^{-1}(0) \oplus (\mathscr{E} - \mathscr{A})^{-1}(0)$.
