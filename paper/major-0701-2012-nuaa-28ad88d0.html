（未标节）三
设 $\mathbb{R}^3$ 的线性变换 $\mathscr{A}$ 使得
$\mathscr{A}\begin{pmatrix} 1 \\ 1 \\ 0 \end{pmatrix} = \begin{pmatrix} 0 \\ -1 \\ 1 \end{pmatrix}, \mathscr{A}\begin{pmatrix} 0 \\ 1 \\ 1 \end{pmatrix} = \begin{pmatrix} 1 \\ a+1 \\ 0 \end{pmatrix}, \mathscr{A}\begin{pmatrix} 1 \\ 1 \\ 1 \end{pmatrix} = \begin{pmatrix} 1 \\ a-1 \\ 1 \end{pmatrix}.$
