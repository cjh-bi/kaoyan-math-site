$\mathscr{A}^2 = \mathscr{A},$
证明:
(1) $\mathscr{A}$ 的特征值只能为 0 或 1.

(3) $\mathscr{A}^{-1}(0)$ 与 $\mathscr{A}(V)$ 都是 $\mathscr{A}$ 的不变子空间.
(4) $V = \mathscr{A}^{-1}(0) \oplus \mathscr{A}(V)$.
