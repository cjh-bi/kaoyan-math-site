（未标节）五
设 $f(x)$ 在 $[0, +\infty)$ 上连续有界, $\varphi(x)$ 在 $[0, +\infty)$ 上连续, 且 $\int_{0}^{+\infty} |\varphi(t)| dt = a < 1$. 构造函数列 $\{g_n(x)\}$ 如下:
$g_0(x) = f(x), \ g_n(x) = f(x) + \int_{0}^{x} \varphi(t) g_{n-1}(t) dt, \ n = 1, \ 2, \ \cdots.$
证明: 函数列 $\{g_n(x)\}$ 在 $[0, +\infty)$ 上一致收敛于连续函数 $g(x)$, 且
$|g(x)| \leqslant \frac{M}{1 - a}.$
这里 $M = \sup_{x \in [0, +\infty)} f(x)$.

（未标节）七
设 $f(x, y)$ 在 $x^{2}+y^{2} \leqslant 1$ 上有连续的二阶偏导数, 且满足:
$\left(\left(\frac{\partial^{2} f}{\partial x^{2}}\right)^{2}+2\left(\frac{\partial^{2} f}{\partial x \partial y}\right)^{2}+\left(\frac{\partial^{2} f}{\partial y^{2}}\right)^{2}\right)(x, y) \leqslant M,(M>0 \text { 为给定的常数) } .$
1. 令 $(u, v, w)=\left(\frac{\partial^{2}}{\partial x^{2}}, \frac{\partial^{2}}{\partial x \partial y} \frac{\partial^{2}}{\partial y^{2}}\right) f(x, y)$, 记 $\overrightarrow{\alpha}=(u, \sqrt{2} v, w), \overrightarrow{\beta}=\left(x^{2}, \sqrt{2} x y, y^{2}\right)$, 证明:
$|\overrightarrow{\alpha} \cdot \overrightarrow{\beta}| \leqslant \sqrt{M}\left(x^{2}+y^{2}\right) .$
2. 若 $f(0,0)=0, \frac{\partial f}{\partial x}(0,0)=0, \frac{\partial f}{\partial y}(0,0)=0$, 证明:
$\left|\iint_{x^{2}+y^{2} \leqslant 1} f(x, y) \mathrm{d} x \mathrm{d} y\right| \leqslant \frac{\pi \sqrt{M}}{4} .$
(提示: 将 $f(x, y)$ 在点 $(0,0)$ 按泰勒级数展开).
