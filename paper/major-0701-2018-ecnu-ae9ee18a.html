$\mathrm{Tip}(W) = \{\mathrm{tip}(\alpha) : \alpha \in W, \alpha \neq 0\}.$
$\mathrm{NonTip}(W) = \{e_1, e_2, \cdots, e_n\} - \mathrm{Tip}(W).$
