$x_n = \sum_{k=1}^{n} \frac{1}{k} - \ln n \ (n = 1, 2, 3, \cdots).$

(1) $\dfrac{1}{n} < \ln\left(1 + \dfrac{1}{n}\right) < \dfrac{1}{n}$;
