设 $v=v(x_1,x_2,\cdots,x_n)$ 是二阶连续可导的 $n$ 元函数. 记
$$\nabla := \left(\frac{\partial}{\partial x_1},\frac{\partial}{\partial x_2},\cdots,\frac{\partial}{\partial x_n}\right),\quad \Delta := \frac{\partial^2}{\partial x_1^2}+\frac{\partial^2}{\partial x_2^2}+\cdots+\frac{\partial^2}{\partial x_n^2}.$$
同时 $D^2v := \left(\dfrac{\partial^2 v}{\partial x_i \partial x_j}\right)_{n\times n}$ 为矩阵, $|D^2v|=\left(\sum_{i,j=1}^{n} v_{x_i x_j}^2\right)^{\frac{1}{2}}$. 证明:
$$\nabla v \cdot \nabla \Delta v = \frac{1}{2}\Delta|\nabla v|^2 - |D^2v|^2.$$
