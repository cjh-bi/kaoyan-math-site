$\mathscr{A}: V \to V, f(x) \mapsto f(x+1) - f(x).$

$\gamma_0 = 1, \gamma_1 = \frac{x}{1}, \gamma_2 = \frac{x(x-1)}{2!}, \cdots, \gamma_{n-1} = \frac{x(x-1)\cdots(x-n+2)}{(n-1)!}$
