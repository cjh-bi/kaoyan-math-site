设 u = u(x,y), v = v(x,y) 由方程 { x = veu + u + v ; y = uev + u − v } 所确定，求 ∂u/∂x、∂v/∂x
查看答案与解析
答案∂u/∂x = (u e^v - 1)/D，∂v/∂x = -(e^v + 1)/D，其中 D = (1 + v e^u)(u e^v - 1) - (1 + e^u)(1 + e^v)
1. 固定 $y$，对 $x = v e^u + u + v$ 和 $y = u e^v + u - v$ 关于 $x$ 求导：\n $(1+v e^u)u_x + (1+e^u)v_x = 1$，\n $(1+e^v)u_x + (u e^v - 1)v_x = 0$。\n2. 系数行列式为 $D = (1+v e^u)(u e^v - 1) - (1+e^u)(1+e^v)$。\n3. 解得 $u_x = (u e^v - 1)/D$，$v_x = -(1+e^v)/D$。
